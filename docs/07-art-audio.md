# 07 · Art and audio

## Visual style

* **Look:** soft anime in the spirit of *Nagi no Asukara*: pastel cel-shaded characters against painterly, detailed backgrounds. Toon shading with a two-step ramp, gentle rim light, painted skies.
* **Light is the star.** Shimmering caustics on every underwater surface, god-rays through the water, dust motes and tiny bubbles drifting, wet-sand reflections at the shore. Underwater is *bright*, not dark.
* **Palette:** the character palette sets the world's: cream skies, sea greens and navies in the water, coral and blush accents for anything alive or important. Sand gold for the human world (wood, rope, lamplight).
* **Shape language:** round and flowing for the sea (shells, waves, kelp); upright and angular for the land (mills, roofs, fences); torn and jagged for the Unfinished.
* **Iridescence** is reserved for things of the sea: Nix's skin, pearls, bubbles, the tide stones.

### Tide lighting
| Tide | Light |
|---|---|
| Ebb | Warm, bright, slightly hazy; long shadows on wet sand |
| Slack | Neutral morning light |
| Flood | Cool, blue-green, reflections everywhere; light shafts through water |

### The Unfinished
Made of torn book pages and sea-foam. Visible handwriting on their bodies that trails off mid-word. They dissolve into foam (Soothe) or burst into paper scraps (Scatter).

### Perlgrund (the sea village)
* Shell-roofed houses, round windows, coral and kelp gardens, strings of glowing lanterns between buildings.
* Fish drift through lanes like birds; laundry made of sea-silk sways in the current.
* Light: warm turquoise by day, lantern gold at night. Changes by season; by winter, sea snow settles on the roofs.

### Shore towns
* Breakwaters, piers, boat sheds, telephone wires and cobbled slopes down to the water. Golden-hour and blue-hour skies.

### Seasons
| Season | Palette |
|---|---|
| Summer | High-key turquoise, white clouds, coral accents |
| Autumn | Amber light, deeper teal water, longer shadows |
| Winter | Pale blue-grey, falling sea snow, warm lantern pools |
| Spring (epilogue) | Soft greens and blush pink |

## Camera

* Close third-person, slightly low (looking up at Nix a little), 4 m default distance.
* A slow, subtle drift as if underwater, even on land (toggle in Accessibility).
* Pulls back to 7 m during Glide and in tide transitions so the player sees the water move.

## Music

* **Ensemble:** piano and strings at the core (the emotional, slightly melancholy anime-score sound), with harp, celesta, glass harmonica and soft wordless voice.
* **Underwater mix:** everything slightly muffled and reverberant below the surface; crossing the waterline crossfades between the shore mix and the sea mix.
* **Layered district themes:** each district theme starts as a single instrument and adds one layer per restored beat. A finished district plays the full arrangement.
* **Tide song:** each district has a short motif heard in Listen mode; it's the melody of that tale.
* **Stance colour:** Soft leans harp and celesta; Strong brings in cello and low percussion. The music cross-fades with the stance switch.

## Sound

* **Breath pops** are pitched to the current key so spending Breath is musical.
* **Footsteps** change with tide: wet sand (Ebb), cobbles (Slack), splashing (Flood).
* **Nix's voice:** no full voice acting; short vocal sounds (a hum, a laugh, a gasp) per expression.
* **The Unfinished** whisper fragments of sentences that stop abruptly.
