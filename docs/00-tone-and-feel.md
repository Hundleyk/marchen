# 00 · Tone and feel

**Touchstone:** *Nagi no Asukara* (A Lull in the Sea, P.A. Works, 2013).

We take its **feeling**, not its story, names or world rules. Märchen by the Sea should feel like that show sounds: quiet, luminous, a little sad, and full of love for a small place by the water.

## The feeling in one line

> *A gentle, bittersweet story about two worlds on either side of the waterline, and the people who love across it while everything slowly changes.*

## What we take

| Feeling | How it shows up in Märchen |
|---|---|
| **Two worlds, one shoreline** | A village under the bay (Nix's home, **Perlgrund**) and the shore towns above it. People from each side are curious about, wary of and drawn to the other. |
| **Luminous underwater life** | The sea village isn't dark or scary. It's bright, warm and lived-in: lanterns, shell roofs, light shafts, fish drifting past windows like birds. |
| **The ache of change** | Nothing stays the same. The sea is slowly cooling; friends grow up; finishing a tale means it *ends*. Restoring things always costs a little. |
| **Ordinary moments matter** | Walking someone home along the pier. Sharing food. Sitting on a seawall at dusk. The game gives time and space to these. |
| **Feelings left unsaid** | Characters often don't say what they mean. Listen mode lets Nix hear what's underneath, but not always act on it. |
| **Festivals and rituals** | Each district builds toward a seaside festival that the stuck tale has been blocking. Finishing the tale lets the festival happen. |
| **Seasons and time** | The game moves through one year, summer to winter. By winter the sea is snowing. |

## What we don't take

* No characters, names, places, terms or plot points from the show.
* No direct copies of its world rules. Our sea-people breathe through the **bubbles on their skin** (our Breath system), which is ours.
* No school-drama structure; our frame is fairy tales.

## Emotional arc

| Season | Districts | Mood |
|---|---|---|
| **Summer** | Nixmill, Fisher's Hollow | Curiosity, first friendships across the waterline, bright days |
| **Autumn** | Swanreach, Undine's Rest | Promises, misunderstandings, the first cold currents |
| **Winter** | The Glass Deep | Sea snow; a choice about what to keep and what to let end |
| **Epilogue: Spring** | The whole coast | Life after the change; who stayed, who left, what was kept |

## Tone rules for writers and artists

1. **Soft light over spectacle.** If a scene could be loud or quiet, make it quiet.
2. **Every ending is a little bittersweet.** Nothing is purely fixed; something is always given up or left behind.
3. **Leave room for silence.** Let dialogue pause. Let the camera rest on the water.
4. **The sea is a place, not a level.** People live there, cook, argue and fall asleep there.
5. **Kindness is strength.** Soft and Strong are both forms of caring; neither is cold.

## Reference board (to collect)

* Underwater light: caustics on sand, god-rays, dust motes in water.
* Seaside towns at golden hour and blue hour: piers, breakwaters, fishing boats, telephone wires against the sky.
* Snow falling into the sea; snow on a calm seabed.
* Pastel cel-shaded characters against painterly, highly detailed backgrounds.
