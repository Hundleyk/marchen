**Target length:** 45–60 minutes · **Covers:** Act 1 · **Teaches:** walking and swimming, diving and Breath, sea-sheen, the market, the tide, words, belonging, the festival

## Walkthrough

### 1. Dawn at the pier (5 min)
* Open underwater: she swims up through bright water toward the light. Title card.
* She surfaces at the end of the pier with her **surfacing whistle**. A fisherman nearly drops his coffee, then waves.
* **Teach:** climbing out, walking on land (slightly unsteady at first), camera.
* The **Harbourmaster** hurries over with a clipboard: he's never registered a merfolk before. He reads her letter, fusses over the right form, gives up, and walks her up the stairs into town himself.
* **First word:** he calls her a *"sea-guest,"* the town's polite old word for merfolk visitors. It goes into the journal.

### 2. The white town (10 min)
* Free walk up Brinehaven's stairs and terraces: white walls, blue doors, laundry lines, cats in doorways, the sea glittering below.
* Small moments: the baker presses a sweet bun into her hands "for the walk"; children follow at a distance, giggling; an old man on a bench tips his hat.
* Her **sea-sheen** starts fading in the sun. A water trough by the fountain teaches the refill. **Ada** appears, laughing, and splashes her with a bucket: "You looked like you needed it."
* **Maren's old house:** empty, with a note on the door: *moved on, no forwarding.* Ada promises to help her ask around.
* Ada tells her where the merfolk live: "the Shelf, past the rocks below the lighthouse."

### 3. The Shelf (8 min)
* She swims around the headland to the cove. Stilt houses, kids diving off rocks, merfolk greeting each other forehead to forehead.
* **Grandmother Wenna** at the **Warming Ring** reads her letter, smiles at Maren's name, then goes quiet: Maren left the Shelf two years ago and hasn't been back.
* Wenna gives her a room above the net-loft (someone has already left a bucket of seawater by the door) and tells her: *"You dive with us tomorrow."*
* **Teach:** journal (People, Words, Customs).

### 4. First dive (12 min)
* Morning, at the turn of the tide. The divers enter the water together with a shared call.
* **Teach:** diving, Breath, depth zones, the surfacing whistle (other divers answer).
* Wenna shows the **divers' rules**: the rested bed (marked with a white stone), the elders' shallows, and how to share a ground.
* Gather abalone and sea-greens on the Pearl Banks' shallow edge. Optional: explore further and find the **sea caves**.
* **Choice:** a big bed sits inside the rested ground. Take from it (Wenna is disappointed) or leave it (Wenna quietly approves).
* Back on shore, the divers sort the harvest together: some for the Shelf's shared pot, some for the market.

### 5. Market and free time (10 min)
* Carry the Shelf's harvest up to the **market square** with Corra. Townsfolk haggle cheerfully; one shopkeeper tries out a merfolk greeting and gets it hilariously wrong.
* Explore at low tide: tidepools and the beach below the cliffs open toward the sea caves.
* Optional: meet **Pim and Pell** and help them find a lost net in the sea caves → learn the words *"saltkin"* and *"deepward."*
* Optional: Ada asks her to teach her a merfolk word. Teaching a word is a small dialogue minigame.

### 6. Evening at the Warming Ring (6 min)
* The divers sit around the fire, warming up and sharing food and news. Wenna tells the story of how the Shelf began.
* She learns the community's customs: the forehead greeting, the shared first catch, the shell set aside for those far away.
* Corra teases her for "going soft for the dry-foots" after one day; Wenna laughs and tells Corra how long *she* took to stop gawping at the town.

### 7. Night: the first light (5 min)
* She goes alone to the end of the pier and lights a single shell from home on the water, the **Lighting of the Water**. A short letter home is written in her voice.
* Ada sees from the lighthouse path and waves. A few windows in town glow back.
* **First barnacle in Brinehaven** grows: a small one, for the town's welcome.
* End of slice: where did Maren go?

## Balance check
About 25 minutes in town (pier, stairs and terraces, market, Warming Ring) and 25 minutes in the water (arrival swim, the swim to the Shelf, the first dive, the sea caves, the night light).

## Teaching order
1. Walk / camera / stairs
2. Swim / surface / dive
3. Sea-sheen (getting wet on land)
4. Journal: people, words, customs
5. Breath and depth; the divers' rules
6. Tide (low tide opens the caves)
7. Teaching and learning words
8. Belonging (first barnacle)
