# 05 · First chapter: Arrival (vertical slice)

**Target length:** 45–60 minutes · **Covers:** Act 1 · **Teaches:** walking and swimming, diving and Breath, dive jobs, the tide, words, belonging, the festival

## Walkthrough

### 1. Dawn at the pier (5 min)
* Open underwater: she swims up through bright, empty water toward the light. Title card.
* She surfaces at the end of the pier with her **surfacing whistle**. Fishermen stare.
* **Teach:** climbing out, walking on land (slightly unsteady at first), camera.
* The **Harbourmaster** stops her: who is she, what's her business? She shows her letter. He doesn't know where the Shelf is, or pretends not to.
* **First word:** a dockworker calls her *"salt-pup"* (a slightly rude name for a young merfolk). It goes into the journal with a note: *not kind.*

### 2. The town (8 min)
* Free walk through Brinehaven's harbour streets. Shops, nets drying, children, gulls.
* Small, everyday moments: a baker who turns her away; a girl (**Ada**) who chases after her to return her dropped letter and asks about her fins.
* The **Company office**: Teodor Vane hires her on the spot as a pearl diver. Pay is posted on the wall: *Human divers: 10 shells. Merfolk divers: 5 shells.* She can comment, or say nothing.
* Ada tells her where the merfolk live: "the Shelf, past the rocks. Everyone knows."

### 3. The Shelf (8 min)
* She swims around the headland to the cove. Stilt houses, kids diving off rocks, merfolk greeting each other forehead to forehead.
* **Grandmother Wenna** at the **Warming Ring** (the shore shelter with a fire pit) reads her letter, recognizes her aunt's name, and goes quiet. Maren hasn't been seen in two years.
* Wenna gives her a room above the net-loft and tells her: *"You dive with us tomorrow. Learn our rules before you learn theirs."*
* **Teach:** journal (People, Words, Customs).

### 4. First dive (12 min)
* Morning, at the turn of the tide. The divers enter the water together with a shared call.
* **Teach:** diving, Breath, depth zones, the surfacing whistle (other divers answer).
* Wenna shows the **divers' rules**: the closed bed (marked with a stone), the elders' shallows, and how to share a ground.
* Job: gather 10 oysters on the Pearl Banks' shallow edge for the Company. Optional: explore further and find the **sea caves**.
* **Choice:** a big bed sits inside the closed ground. Take from it (more pay, Wenna disappointed) or leave it.
* Back on the pier, the Company weighs her haul and pays her 5 shells. Rafe, a human diver, gets 10 for the same.

### 5. Afternoon free time (10 min)
* Explore at low tide: tidepools and sandbars open toward the sea caves.
* Optional: meet **Pim and Pell** (merfolk twins) and help them find a lost net in the sea caves → learn the words *"saltkin"* and *"deepward."*
* Optional: meet **Corra**, who's angry at the town and at the hero for taking a Company job.
* Ada finds her again and asks her to teach her a merfolk word. Teaching a word is a small dialogue minigame.

### 6. Evening at the Warming Ring (6 min)
* The divers sit around the fire, warming up and sharing food and news. Wenna tells the story of how the Shelf was founded.
* She learns the community's customs: the forehead greeting, the shared first catch, the shell set aside for those far away.
* Corra and the hero argue about the Company. Wenna ends it.

### 7. Night: the first light (5 min)
* She goes alone to the end of the pier and lights a single shell from home on the water, the **Lighting of the Water**. A short letter home is written in her voice.
* Ada sees from the lighthouse path and waves.
* **First barnacle in Brinehaven** grows: a small one, for Wenna's welcome.
* End of slice: the question of her aunt.

## Teaching order
1. Walk / camera
2. Swim / surface / dive
3. Breath and depth
4. Journal: people, words, customs
5. Dive job and the divers' rules
6. Tide (low tide opens the caves)
7. Teaching and learning words
8. Belonging (first barnacle)
