# 05 · Level: Nixmill (vertical slice)

**Target length:** 20–30 minutes · **Teaches:** movement, Breath, tide stones, Soft/Strong, Listen, Soothe/Scatter · **Reward:** Comb shell (Glide)

## Layout

```
                 N
        [Bramble Hill]──────[Mill & Wheel]
              │                   │
   (sea) ─[Landing Beach]──[Fallen Bridge]──[Village Square]
              │                   │                │
        [Tidepools]         [The Channel]     [Glass Lake]
                                  │
                           [The Nixie's Well]
```

* **Size:** about 180 × 140 m.
* **Tide stones:** one at the Landing Beach (tutorial), one at the Fallen Bridge.
* **Water height reference:** Slack = 0 m (village street level is +0.5 m).

## Walkthrough

### 0. Arrival: Landing Beach (2 min)
* Opening: a wave carries Nix onto the sand at dawn. Bubbles cover their skin (full Breath).
* **Teach:** WASD, camera, jump. A dry sand stretch to walk up shows bubbles popping lightly while running (soft demo of Breath, no cost yet).
* **Teach Breath:** a rock gap needs a float-jump hang. Breath drops; walking back into the surf refills it. Text prompt: *"The sea fills you."*

### 1. Tidepools: first tide stone (3 min)
* A tide stone on the beach. Prompt to use it in Soft → tide lowers to Ebb.
* Tidepools open; a pearl and the first Foamling. **Teach Soothe** (hold E).
* **Teach stance switch:** a brittle sea wall blocks the route north. Prompt Q → Strong → Surge through.

### 2. Fallen Bridge and Village Square (4 min)
* Meet **Mira**, the miller's daughter. She's the only one not frozen; she noticed the siblings never came home.
* **Teach Listen** (hold F): three threads glow across town: the brush, the comb, the mirror.
* Player chooses order freely from here.

### 3. Beat: The Thrown Brush, Bramble Hill (5 min)
* The brush is frozen above a hill of giant brambles that grew from it.
* **Soft route:** raise the tide (needs Strong at the bridge stone), swim the flooded mill-race to the hill's back, then thread a path through the thorns while the Foamlings drift past. Soothe them one by one.
* **Strong route:** Surge through the bramble walls (brittle), scatter Foamlings; faster, but Page-gulls harass.
* **Restoration:** the brush lands; the brambles relax into a hedge.

### 4. Beat: The Thrown Comb, The Channel (5 min)
* The comb hangs over a deep channel; its frozen teeth became stone spikes along the banks.
* **Soft route:** lower the tide to Ebb, walk the channel bed, float-jump up and catch the comb.
* **Strong route:** raise to Flood, swim against the current, wave-push the comb free (wave push is borrowed for this beat only, a preview of Fisher's Hollow).
* **Restoration:** the spikes sink; the comb shell glints on the bed.

### 5. Beat: The Thrown Mirror, Glass Lake (5 min)
* The lake is a sheet of glass; the mirror hangs above its centre. Inkwells spread across the glass.
* **Soft route:** lower the tide so Inkwells shrink to cores; soothe each core.
* **Strong route:** raise the tide; the glass floats up in plates; Surge across the plates.
* **Restoration:** the glass melts back to water.

### 6. Finale: The Nixie's Well (6 min)
* Three restored throws open the well. The siblings appear, frozen mid-run. The **Nixie** (the Stopped for this district) rises.
* **Encounter:** the Nixie mirrors Nix's stance. In Soft she pleads, in Strong she rages.
  * **Soft resolution:** hold Listen to hear her story (she was lonely); answer three dialogue prompts to complete her sentence.
  * **Strong resolution:** between her wave patterns, Surge to break three glass pages floating around her.
* **Ending vignette:** the siblings run home; the Nixie stays as the well's keeper (Soft) or returns to the river (Strong).
* **Reward:** Comb shell joins the necklace → Glide unlocked. The tide in Nixmill starts cycling.

## Collectibles
* 10 pearls (3 in the Tidepools, 2 on Bramble Hill, 2 on the channel bed, 2 on Glass Lake, 1 on the mill roof at Flood).
* 2 keepsakes (Strong routes) and 2 tale memories (Soft routes).

## Tutorial prompts (in order)
1. Move / camera
2. Jump, hold to float
3. Walk into the sea to refill Breath
4. Use tide stone (Soft lowers)
5. Hold E to soothe
6. Q to switch nature; Shift to Surge
7. Hold F to listen
8. Tide stone in Strong raises
